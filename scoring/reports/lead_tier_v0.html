Checks and caveats

Engine parity. The current rubric, run through the new scoring engine on historical answers, reproduces the score stored at submit time on 7,329 of 7,329 leads and the tier on 7,329 of 7,329. The engine that produced this report is the one that ships in the form.

Method. exam 0.705, fitted 0.712 on the validation month (2026-05-16 to 2026-06-15); "fitted" was chosen there, without looking at the test window.

Rep behavior. Tiers were visible to reps and drove calendar routing until 2026-10-09, so historical rates partly reflect treatment. Leads since then saw no tier; once they are 30 days old they are a cleaner check.

Thin questions. Three newer questions were asked of only a few hundred baseline leads, so they carry small caps until more data accumulates.
